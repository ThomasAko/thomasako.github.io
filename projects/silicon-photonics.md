# Integrated silicon photonics (PhD)

*Ghent University / IMEC · PhD in Electrical Engineering*

**Problem.** Building compact, tunable optical components on silicon chips.

**What I did.**
- Designed electro-optic and thermo-optic integrated silicon photonic components, including devices using liquid crystals
- Characterized the fabricated chips in the lab: optical test setups, measurement automation and data analysis
- Continued at IMEC as a photonics test engineer on silicon nitride photonics for life-science applications

**Role.** Doctoral researcher; first author on the main publications (2010–2015).
