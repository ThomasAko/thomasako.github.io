# Moirae / auto-soh: open-source battery health estimation

*Argonne National Laboratory · open source · 2025–present*

**Problem.** Estimating state of health from field data requires reliable equivalent-circuit models, and those models have to be extracted from messy cycler data first.

**What I did.**
- Main maintainer of [Moirae (ROVI-org/auto-soh)](https://github.com/ROVI-org/auto-soh) since 2026
- Worked on the robustness of offline parameter extraction: assembler fitting and the hysteresis model
- Used Moirae as the simulation backbone for the [joint estimation study](ukf-joint-estimation.md)
